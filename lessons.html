Formats

Four ways to book.

1:1

Private

The coach’s full attention for the whole session. The fastest way to fix technique.

2:1

Semi-private

Two swimmers, one coach. Ideal for siblings or training partners.

3:1

Small group

Three swimmers at a similar level, with feedback for each one.

4:1

Group

Four swimmers, still small enough for individual corrections.
