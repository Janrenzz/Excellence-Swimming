What clinics cover

Built around the details that decide races.

Each clinic focuses on a few race skills, taught in the water by athletes who use them at the highest level.

{{icon:arrow-down}} Starts & underwaters

Explosive starts, tight streamlines and strong underwater kicks that set up the whole race.

{{icon:waves}} Turns & breakouts

Open and crossover turns, pullouts and breakouts that carry speed off every wall.

{{icon:target}} Race detail & finishes

Stroke efficiency, race awareness and finishes, with individual correction from the coaches.
